Your 55 in TV sets where the sofa goes

  • A 55 in looks right from 2.1 to 2.8 m. In the alcove layout that pulls the sofa about 1.9 m off the back wall, measured to the sofa's back.
  • That frees a strip behind it: a console against the sofa back and a sideboard on the back wall still leave 110 cm to walk between the doors.
  • A 140 cm unit suits a 55 in and leaves 16 cm each side in the 172 cm alcove.
  • The shelves, console and sideboard are suggestions. Remove them or add an armchair, lamp or desk with the buttons.

Sofa: up to 290 cm long, chaise on the right

  • A long side up to 290 cm still leaves 90 cm or more past the pier to the kitchen. The chaise can run to about 170 cm.
  • A chaise on your right as you sit is usually listed as left-hand facing, because brands name it as you stand facing the sofa. Check each brand's diagram.
  • Sofa bed: the mattress comes out about 120 cm past the seat. In the alcove layout it stops about 15 cm short of the hearth once the coffee table is moved. Tick "Open the sofa bed" to see it.
  • Rug: 160 × 230 cm in the alcove layout, 200 × 290 cm facing the right wall. Both are stock sizes.
