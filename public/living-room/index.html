Sofa + ottoman: lounge at the front, guest bed behind

  • The 3-seater sits 2.2 m from your 55 in, which leaves a 227 cm strip behind it: exactly a 137 cm bed plus a 90 cm walkway between the back doors.
  • Everyday, the Portland (137 × 80 cm closed) sits against the sofa back as a bench. For guests, turn it 90° and it unfolds to 137 × 216 cm behind the sofa. The coffee table stays put.
  • With the bed open there's still 90 cm along the back for the bathroom and your bedroom at night, and 91 cm past the pier to the kitchen. The guest gets in from the left end.
  • The back wall has to stay clear for that, so the sideboard moves to the left wall between the kitchen opening and the pier (110 × 40 cm).
  • The coffee table is 110 × 50 cm so it keeps about 36 cm to the sofa and to the TV unit. Tick "Guests staying" to see the guest setup.

Other sofas and sizes

  • Switch the sofa type to try a chaise, corner, L-shaped, 2, 3 or 4-seater in any layout. Straight sofas centre on the TV, corner types keep their corner.
  • A chaise or corner sofa's long side can go to 290 cm and still leave 90 cm past the pier to the kitchen.
  • A chaise on your right as you sit is usually listed as left-hand facing, because brands name it as you stand facing the sofa.
  • Tick "Guests staying" to see every bed unfolded: the ottoman, and the sofa's pull-out if you mark it as a sofa bed.
  • A 140 cm unit suits a 55 in and leaves 16 cm each side in the 172 cm alcove. Rugs: 160 × 230 or 200 × 290 cm are stock sizes.
